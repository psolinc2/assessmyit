The three tiers of IT support

The tiers differ by what you can ask your provider to show, not by tool names or marketing.

You can check every row below against your own contract, invoices and last quarter's reports. Offshore staff don't disqualify a provider on their own. What matters is who can touch privileged data, how that access is logged, and who is accountable.

A firm can sit in different tiers on different rows. The scorecard measures that.

Price ranges are estimates for firms of 1-50 users in Los Angeles and vary with scope, on-site needs and the age of your systems.
