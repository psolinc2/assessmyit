About

Who built this

I'm Ari, owner of Promising Solutions, an IT and cybersecurity firm in Los Angeles. I built this site, and I'm telling you that up front because you should know who is behind a scorecard before you trust it.

I've spent about 30 years in IT, and most of it around law firms. I ran IT in-house for almost a decade, and I've run an MSP for more than 20 years. In that time I've seen firms of the same size pay very different prices for very different service, with no easy way to tell which one they were getting.

Why it exists

Most managed service providers are not bad. Most are doing what the contract says. The problem is that the contract often says very little, and the firm has no way to check. Backups are reported as running, but nobody has restored anything. A disaster recovery plan exists on paper, but nobody has walked through it. The firm finds out what it was missing on the worst day.

The scorecard asks 24 questions that you can answer from your own contract, invoices and reports. It doesn't name or rate any provider. It tells you where your current setup is strong, where it's thin, and what to ask for.

What I do with your answers

Your answers are used to build your report. I will not sell your information or share it with anyone. If you ask me to look at your contract and invoice, I'll send you a written review and nothing else unless you ask for more.

Full disclosure

Promising Solutions provides managed IT services to law firms. If your score shows gaps and you want help, I'd be glad to help. If you'd rather take the report back to your current provider and get those items fixed, that's a fine outcome, and the report is yours either way.
