Your IT company says everything is fine. Can they prove it?

Most IT providers aren't bad. Most do what the contract says. The problem is that the contract often says very little, and you have no way to check.

This is a 24-question scorecard for law firms, written for managing partners. You don't need your IT person to answer. It takes about 10 minutes. It doesn't name or rate any provider.

Take the scorecard

What it checks

  • Would you know if something was broken, and would you hear back when you need help?
  • Could this hurt a client matter: ransomware, a lost file, a case management outage?
  • If a client or insurer asked for proof of your security tomorrow, could you show it?
  • Do you control your own accounts, and could you switch providers without starting over?
  • Are you paying for things you're not getting?

Three kinds of IT support

Firms of the same size pay very different prices for very different service. In plain terms: a Basic provider handles tickets. A Solid provider gives you good day-to-day support and some reporting. A White glove provider also plans ahead, tests recovery, and gives you evidence for clients and insurers. See what each should provide.
