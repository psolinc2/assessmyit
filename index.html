Three kinds of IT support

Firms of the same size pay very different prices for very different service. In plain terms: a Basic provider handles tickets. A Solid provider gives you good day-to-day support and some reporting. A White glove provider also plans ahead, tests recovery, and gives you evidence for clients and insurers. See what each should provide.
